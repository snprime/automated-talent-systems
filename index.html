What this comparison can—and cannot—tell you

The limitation. A vendor’s architecture description and a city’s oversight rules answer different questions. Neither demonstrates that a particular applicant received a fair outcome.

An audit is not an outcome guarantee. NYC’s FAQ says the law does not require specific actions based on audit results. Other anti-discrimination laws still apply.

A perspective easy to miss. Someone who is never surfaced during sourcing may never become an applicant. Consider their visibility alongside the experience of people already being assessed.
